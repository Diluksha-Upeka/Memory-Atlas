I am increasingly obsessed with interfaces that explain themselves without needing a lecture.
The feeling I want is clarity: a page that makes the important direction obvious at a glance.
I keep coming back to the idea that design should feel calm, simple, and readable.
What I admire lately are tools that reduce cognitive friction and make thought feel visible.
I like the way software can become a personal map for how a mind works.
The work I want to make is expressive but not cluttered, useful but still alive.
